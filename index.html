What the work finds

One venue. $70,000 a year. Three ordinary causes.

The engagement

A single-site venue turning over about $1M a year came to us profitable on paper and short of cash. Reading the month rather than the year, we found up to $70,000 a year leaving through three ordinary things: shift design that put staff on before the trade arrived, food waste nobody was measuring, and trading hours that cost more to stay open than they brought in.

That is one venue and one engagement, not a promise. Your number will be different. The way we find it will not.

What we read, every month

  • Wage cost against sales, worked back to the roster
  • Food and beverage cost, and the drift behind it
  • Prime cost, wages and stock together
  • Rent and overhead as a share of what you actually take
  • What changed since last month, and what it is worth over a year

Benchmarks we work to: wages at or under 30% of sales, food and beverage at or under 36%, prime cost at or under 62%, rent at or under 10%.
