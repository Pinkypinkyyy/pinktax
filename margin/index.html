What the monthly read actually answers

Four questions, every month, in plain English — so a bad month gets caught in the month, not in July.

01

Did you make money?

Real profit for the month on a proper accrual read, not what the bank balance suggests.

02

What changed?

Against last month and the same month last year, so drift shows up while you can still act on it.

03

Where is it leaking?

Wages, food and beverage, rent and overhead, each against what it should be for a venue like yours.

04

A short list of changes worth making, ranked, with the dollars attached to each one.
